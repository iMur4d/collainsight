# Problem Statement

## Executive Summary

Modern organizations generate valuable knowledge every day through conversations.

Ideas, suggestions, discussions, feedback, and technical questions are constantly exchanged across communication platforms such as Telegram.

However, while communication is fast, knowledge management remains fragmented.

The result is a growing amount of valuable information that becomes increasingly difficult to discover, reuse, or transform into actionable decisions.

The platform addresses this gap by turning conversations into structured organizational knowledge.

---

# Current Challenges

Teams face several recurring problems during daily collaboration.

### Knowledge Loss

Valuable ideas disappear inside long chat histories.

Even when an idea is important, finding it later becomes difficult.

---

### Information Overload

Managers and team leads often receive hundreds of messages every day.

Reading every conversation is time-consuming and reduces decision-making efficiency.

---

### Repeated Questions

The same questions are frequently asked because previous answers are difficult to discover.

Knowledge exists, but accessibility is poor.

---

### Fragmented Collaboration

Team members often work on similar ideas without realizing it.

Potential collaboration opportunities remain hidden.

---

### Lack of Organizational Memory

Most conversations are temporary.

Organizations rarely build a persistent knowledge base from their daily communication.

---

# Root Causes

These problems exist because communication tools are designed for messaging rather than knowledge management.

Messages are stored chronologically rather than semantically.

Important ideas become buried beneath newer conversations.

There is little understanding of relationships between discussions, projects, or contributors.

---

# Impact

These challenges affect both individuals and organizations.

For individuals:

- Lost ideas
- Reduced productivity
- Poor knowledge organization

For teams:

- Duplicate work
- Weak collaboration
- Repeated discussions

For management:

- Information overload
- Slow decision making
- Limited visibility into team knowledge

---

# Existing Solutions

Many organizations currently rely on combinations of:

- Telegram
- WhatsApp
- Slack
- Notion
- Obsidian
- Google Docs

Each tool solves part of the problem.

Communication platforms enable discussion.

Knowledge management platforms enable documentation.

However, there is little automation connecting both worlds.

---

# Why Existing Solutions Are Not Enough

Current tools expect users to manually organize knowledge.

This creates additional effort and often results in incomplete documentation.

As conversations increase, manual organization becomes unrealistic.

Artificial intelligence should perform this transformation automatically.

---

# Opportunity

Advances in Large Language Models now make it possible to understand conversations instead of simply storing them.

Rather than preserving raw messages, organizations can continuously build structured knowledge from daily communication.

This creates a living organizational memory that improves over time.

---

# Success Criteria

The platform succeeds when:

- Valuable ideas are no longer lost.
- Managers spend less time reading conversations.
- Team knowledge becomes searchable.
- Collaboration opportunities become visible.
- Organizational knowledge grows automatically.
- AI assists knowledge management without increasing user effort.
